Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains what information Dose Drill uses, why, and your rights under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). Dose Drill is the controller of personal data described here. Contact: aryanamin1361@gmail.com.

1. The short version

We collect as little as possible. Your practice progress stays on your device. Payments are handled by Stripe. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

2. Information stored on your device

Your XP, level, streak, scores, saved mistakes, flashcard progress and premium status are saved in your browser's local storage. This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time from Account › Reset all progress, or by clearing your browser data.

3. Payment information

When you subscribe, Stripe collects your name, email, card details and billing country to take payment and prevent fraud. Stripe shares with us your email, the amount paid, and the date and status of your subscription, so we can manage billing and refunds. We never receive your full card number. Stripe uses your data under its own privacy policy at stripe.com/gb/privacy.

4. The Help assistant

The Help assistant runs inside the app. Messages you type are used only to show you answers on your device and are not stored by us. If you send a refund request, the details you choose to include (your checkout email, the reason and any notes) are sent to us by email so a person can review the request.

5. Why we use your information

  • To provide the app and your subscription (to carry out our contract with you).
  • To process payments, refunds and billing questions (contract, and our legal duty to keep financial records).
  • To prevent fraud and misuse (our legitimate interest in protecting the app).

6. How long we keep it

We keep refund request emails for up to 2 years. Payment records are kept for 6 years, as UK tax law requires. On-device data stays until you delete it.

7. Hosting

The app is hosted on claude.ai, a platform run by Anthropic. When you open the app, Anthropic may process technical information such as your IP address and device type to deliver the page securely, under its own privacy policy.

8. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to data portability. To use these rights, contact us at the email above. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Children

The app is designed for adults in pharmacy training. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
